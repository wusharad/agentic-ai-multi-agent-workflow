# Atos – Human Resources Policy

## Leave Policy
- All full-time employees are entitled to **20 paid leaves per calendar year**.
- Leave balance resets every January.
- Employees on probation are entitled to **10 paid leaves**.
- Leave requests must be applied through the HR portal and approved by the reporting manager.

## Sick Leave
- Employees can avail up to **10 sick leaves per year**.
- Medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days.

## Work From Home Policy
- Employees may work from home up to **2 days per week** with manager approval.
- Extended work-from-home arrangements require HR approval.

## Escalation
For HR-related clarifications, employees may contact the HR department via email or raise a request in the HR helpdesk system.
