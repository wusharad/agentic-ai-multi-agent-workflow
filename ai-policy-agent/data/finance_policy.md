# Atos – Finance Policy

## Expense Reimbursement Policy
- Employees are eligible for reimbursement of official expenses incurred during business activities.
- Supported categories include:
  - Laptop and IT equipment
  - Travel and accommodation
  - Client meeting expenses

## Laptop Reimbursement
- Laptop reimbursement is capped at **₹60,000**.
- Reimbursement claims must be submitted within **30 days of purchase**.
- Original invoice is mandatory.
- Processing time is **15 working days** after submission.

## Travel Reimbursement
- Travel reimbursements must follow Atos-approved vendors and rates.
- Claims must be submitted within **14 days of travel completion**.

## Escalation
If reimbursements are delayed beyond the defined timeline, employees may escalate the issue to the Finance department.
