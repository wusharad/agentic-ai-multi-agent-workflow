# Atos – IT Policy

## IT Asset Policy
- All IT assets (laptops, monitors, peripherals) are company property.
- Employees are responsible for the safe use of assigned assets.

## Laptop Issues
- Hardware or software issues must be reported through the IT Service Desk.
- Replacement laptops are provided within **3 business days** for critical failures.

## Security Policy
- Company laptops must be protected with strong passwords.
- Installation of unauthorized software is strictly prohibited.
- Data must not be copied to personal devices without approval.

## Escalation
For unresolved IT issues or security incidents, employees may escalate to the IT department.
